# Source coverage

## Included

- **40 previous-exam MCQs**: 20 from the 2024 Traffic Supervisor exam and 20 from the 2024 Traffic & Operations Chief exam.
- **60 question-bank MCQs**: extracted from `Rule book questions.zip` after exact-text deduplication.
- Source names, role, topic, and confidence label are stored with each MCQ. Answer letters from candidate papers are deliberately not stored.

## Excluded from automatic scoring

- Question-bank items whose correct-answer text conflicted between duplicate source documents.
- Visual questions whose referenced diagram was not reliably linked to the question.
- Blank or incomplete visual-answer choices.

The previous Traffic Supervisor image question is included with the original technical symbol clipped from the scanned source page. All answers remain unverified until checked against the latest controlled operational document.
